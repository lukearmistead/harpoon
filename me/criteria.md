plainly if it is off-thesis. Talking me out of a bad application beats writing
a good one.

## Keeping this file short

Under 100 lines, standing rules only. Dated history, override counts and open
questions belong in `pipeline.md`, company reasoning in `applications/`. A
rule needing a date or a company name to make sense is a board entry in
disguise.
